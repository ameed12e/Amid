About Me

Where strategy meets execution

I'm Amid Alaiyan — an event planner, production manager, and digital strategist with over 8 years of hands-on experience delivering high-stakes projects across international markets including Toronto and Jerusalem.

My foundation is in live events and commercial production — orchestrating everything from intimate VIP gatherings to large-scale corporate summits. I manage vendors, coordinate cross-functional teams, negotiate contracts, and ensure flawless on-site execution under pressure. Every event I produce is treated as a cinematic experience: intentional, polished, and memorable.

Beyond the stage, I bring full digital capability — building the websites, managing the brand assets, and creating the video content that makes every event live beyond the day itself. As an Amazon seller and e-commerce operator, I also bring sharp commercial instincts: sourcing, logistics, financial tracking, and market analysis are part of my everyday toolkit.

Fluent in Arabic, English, and Hebrew with working Russian — I navigate multilingual environments with ease, making me a natural fit for international programs and cross-cultural stakeholder management.
